## Task 001: gpmd wasm upload command not exiting connection when complete

**Problem**

gpmd wasm upload command not exiting connection when an upload is successful

**Cause**

background/background.go - HandleBackgroundConnection()
- When an upload was complete, the connection did not write or end the connection.

**Solution**
Write that the module had been uploaded successfully and close the connection.
